This checklist covers the ordinary app release. The separate password vault is [deferred](VAULT_RELEASE_MILESTONE.md); do not enable it or migrate personal data for these checks. Keep verification local and use an isolated instance and synthetic prompts, recordings and pictures where possible. Do not download models, switch to cloud providers, or send personal conversations to another service without the owner's explicit choice.
